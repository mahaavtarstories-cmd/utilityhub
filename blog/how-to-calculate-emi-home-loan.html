How to Calculate EMI for Home Loan — Easy Guide

Understand EMI calculation with simple formula, real examples, and a free calculator.

What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed amount you pay every month to repay a loan. It includes both principal and interest, spread evenly across the loan tenure.

EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n - 1]
  • P = Principal loan amount
  • r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = Loan tenure in months

Example Calculation

Let's say you take a home loan of:

  • Loan amount: ₹5,00,000
  • Interest rate: 8.5% per year
  • Tenure: 60 months (5 years)

Monthly rate = 8.5 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.00708
EMI = 5,00,000 × 0.00708 × (1.00708)^60 ÷ [(1.00708)^60 - 1]
EMI ≈ ₹10,268/month
Total payment = ₹6,16,080
Total interest = ₹1,16,080

Factors That Affect Your EMI

  • Loan amount: Higher amount = higher EMI
  • Interest rate: Even 0.5% difference can save lakhs over 20 years
  • Tenure: Longer tenure = lower EMI but more total interest
  • Down payment: Larger down payment = smaller loan = lower EMI
  • Prepayments: Partial prepayments reduce principal and total interest

Tips to Reduce Your EMI

  • Negotiate for lower interest rates — compare 3+ banks
  • Make prepayments whenever possible (even 1 extra EMI/year helps)
  • Choose shorter tenure if you can afford higher EMI
  • Improve your credit score (750+) for better rates
  • Consider balance transfer to a bank offering lower rates
