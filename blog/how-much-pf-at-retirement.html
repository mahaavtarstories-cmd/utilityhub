How Much PF Will I Get at Retirement?

Understand EPF calculation and estimate your retirement corpus with employee + employer contributions and compound interest.

How EPF Works

Every month, you contribute 12% of your basic salary to EPF. Your employer contributes 3.67% (to EPF) + 8.33% (to EPS pension). The EPF portion earns compound interest (currently 8.25%/year) tax-free until withdrawal.

EPF Contribution Breakdown

  • Employee: 12% of basic salary → goes to EPF
  • Employer: 3.67% of basic → goes to EPF
  • Employer: 8.33% of basic → goes to EPS (pension, max ₹1,250/month)
  • Total EPF contribution/month: 15.67% of your basic salary

Example: ₹25,000 Basic Salary, 30 Years

Monthly EPF = ₹25,000 × 15.67% = ₹3,918
With 8.25% interest and 5% annual salary hikes over 30 years:
Total employee contribution: ~₹35 lakhs
Total employer contribution: ~₹11 lakhs
Interest earned: ~₹65 lakhs
Total at retirement: ~₹1.1 crore (tax-free!)

Factors That Affect Your PF Corpus

  • Basic salary: Higher basic = more contribution
  • Years of service: More years = more compounding
  • Interest rate: Government revises annually (8-8.5% range)
  • Salary hikes: Annual hikes increase contributions
  • Withdrawals: Partial withdrawals reduce final corpus

Tips to Maximize Your PF

  • Don't withdraw PF when changing jobs — transfer it instead
  • Voluntary PF contribution (VPF) can boost your corpus significantly
  • Keep basic salary as high as possible in your CTC structure
  • Stay invested for the full tenure — compounding works magic in later years
