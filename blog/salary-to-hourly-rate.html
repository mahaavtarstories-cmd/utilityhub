Convert your annual salary to hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly rates. Know your true value.

Why Convert Salary to Hourly?

When you're offered ₹6,00,000/year, it sounds great. But what does that mean per hour? Knowing your hourly rate helps you: compare job offers, negotiate freelance rates, calculate overtime value, and understand if you're being paid fairly.

Simple Conversion Formulas

Monthly = Annual ÷ 12
Weekly = Annual ÷ 52
Daily = Weekly ÷ Working days per week
Hourly = Weekly ÷ Working hours per week

Example: ₹6,00,000 Annual Salary

Monthly: ₹50,000
Weekly: ₹11,538
Daily (5 days/week): ₹2,308
Hourly (40 hours/week): ₹288
After tax (approx): ₹44,000/month → ₹220/hour

Tips for Salary Negotiation

  • Always know your hourly rate before negotiating
  • Compare with industry benchmarks on Glassdoor/Payscale
  • Factor in benefits — PF, gratuity, health insurance add 15-20% to CTC
  • Negotiate on CTC, not just take-home — tax structure matters
  • For freelancing: multiply hourly rate by 1.5-2x for overhead + taxes
